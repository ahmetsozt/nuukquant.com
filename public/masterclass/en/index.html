Curriculum

The Masterclass curriculum

Six modules, from zero to advanced. From your first candlestick chart to your own system coded with AI.

  1. 01 Finance FundamentalsLearn how markets work and what actually moves prices. ~8 hours
    • Markets and products: forex, gold, indices, crypto and CFDs
    • How spread, swap, lot size and leverage really work
    • Reading the macro picture: Fed decisions, interest rates, inflation, the dollar index (DXY) and bond yields
    • The economic calendar, data days and trading sessions
    • Liquidity and volatility: when and why prices speed up
    • Core finance maths: returns, compounding and expected value

    By the end of the module: your own market watch dashboard and a weekly macro note.

  2. 02 Risk and Position ManagementLearn to protect your capital first; growth comes after. ~6 hours
    • Risk per trade and the right position size
    • Setting stops with ATR, R-multiples and the risk/reward ratio
    • Drawdown maths: why recovering a 50% loss takes a 100% gain
    • Correlation: the combined risk of open positions
    • Keeping a trade journal and measuring performance
    • Trading psychology: sticking to your rules

    By the end of the module: written risk rules and an automatic position size calculator.

  3. 03 Algorithmic TradingTurn an idea into rules, rules into code, and code into tests. ~12 hours
    • Turning a strategy idea into measurable rules
    • Backtesting: data quality, trading costs, slippage and spread
    • Overfitting, look-ahead bias and survivorship bias
    • Walk-forward analysis and out-of-sample testing
    • How a trading robot (EA) for MT4/MT5 is structured, and research in Python
    • Live testing on a demo account and the checklist before going live

    By the end of the module: your first strategy, backtested and walk-forward validated.

  4. 04 Gann LabStrip Gann of the myths and test him against data. ~8 hours
    • W.D. Gann's approach to price and time, and why traders still use it
    • The Square of 9: square-root maths and angle rotations
    • Gann angles and the Gann fan: 1×1, 2×1, 1×2
    • Time cycles and the balance of price and time
    • Coding Gann levels as an indicator
    • Backtesting Gann rules: which ones work and which don't

    By the end of the module: your own Gann indicator and level rules tested on historical data.

  5. 05 Coding with AI (Vibecoding)You don't need to memorise code; learn to explain exactly what you want. ~12 hours
    • A working routine with Claude Code and Cursor
    • Writing a spec that tells the AI everything about your system
    • Getting AI to build trading robots, indicators and market scanners
    • Building a backtesting engine and a performance dashboard
    • Alert bots, data feeds and automation
    • Security: protecting API keys, testing code and fixing bugs

    By the end of the module: a working trading robot built with AI and your personal trading dashboard.

  6. 06 Your Own System Final projectBring everything you've learned together in one system. 30 days
    • Designing the system: idea, rules, risk and infrastructure
    • Coding and backtesting it
    • 30 days of live testing on a demo account
    • A live monitoring dashboard and alerts
    • Presenting your system in a live session and getting feedback

    By the end of the module: a tested trading system of your own that you monitor from your own dashboard.

One of the tools from Module 4

Gann Lab: Square of 9

Enter a price to see possible support and resistance levels from Gann's square-root method. In the module you'll learn to code these levels and test them on historical data.

Use a dot or a comma for decimals; don't use thousands separators. Prices below 10 (for example 1.0850) are calculated in pips.

This tool is for teaching purposes only; it is not a trading signal or investment advice.

      FAQ
      
          
      Frequently asked questions
      
        
      
        
      
          
      I'm a complete beginner. Will this be too hard?
      No. Module 1 starts from the very basics and the topics build in order. You finish every module with something concrete, so you always know where you are.
      
          
      Do I need to know how to code?
      No. That's exactly what the coding with AI module is for: you learn to tell the AI what you want, test the code it writes and run it safely. If you already code, you'll move faster.
      
          
      How much time should I set aside each week?
      Three to four hours a week is enough to keep up with the lessons and live sessions. The demo test in the final project runs on its own; you just check the results each day.
      
          
      What software do I need?
      MetaTrader 4 or 5, Python, VS Code and an AI coding tool (Claude Code or Cursor). All of them have a free version or a trial. The monthly fee for the AI tool is not included.
      
          
      Do you give signals or manage my money?
      No. This is an education programme. NUUK does not accept money for investment, does not manage accounts and does not promise profits. Our aim is for you to make your own decisions and build your own system.
      
          
      Does Gann really work?
      Some Gann rules give statistically meaningful results in some markets, and some don't. In the Gann Lab you learn to test these rules rather than believe them, and to drop the ones that don't work.
      
          
      Are the lessons live or recorded?
      Both. You watch the recorded lessons at your own pace, and every week we go through the market and the code together in a live session and answer your questions.
      
          
      How do I enrol?
      Tap "Reserve your place" and you'll be chatting with us directly on WhatsApp. Your message opens ready to send. "Get the book" works the same way.
      
          
      How do I pay?
      For now we only accept crypto (USDT). When you message us on WhatsApp, we'll send you our USDT wallet address.
      
          
      Is it a one-time payment?
      Yes. You pay $499 once; there is no monthly or yearly fee. The modules, templates, WhatsApp community and live sessions are all included.
